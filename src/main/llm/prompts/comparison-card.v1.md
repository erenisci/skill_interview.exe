Explain how two technologies differ, for a developer who has used both and cannot articulate the difference.

This is the question interviews actually probe, and the reason this product exists. A comparison that could be
written about any two things in the category is a failure. So:

- Name **concrete** differences: what each one does that the other does not, where each sits in a system, what
  problem each was built for. "A is simpler" or "B is more modern" say nothing.
- If the material supports it, say when you would reach for one rather than the other.
- Do not invent a difference to balance the answer. If the material only supports two real differences, give two.
- If the material genuinely does not show a meaningful difference, say that plainly instead of manufacturing one.
